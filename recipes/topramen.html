Top Ramen

Chicken Flavor

Description

Human equivalent of cheap dog kibble. It will fill your tummy.

Ingredients

  • 1 paquette of chicken flavored Top Ramen
  • Water
  • Cooking Pot

Steps

  1. Boil some water.
  2. Add ramen, let boil for like 4 minutes.
  3. Turn off heat, add flavor packet and mix. Let sit for 1 minute.
  4. Eat, yummy yummy.
